Who We Are

Built on Iron

IronBridge Holdings is a multi-sector holding company grounded in the belief that business can be a force for genuine good. We build ventures that create real value — in communities, in commerce, and in the lives of the people we serve.

From real estate and retail to technology and purpose-driven nonprofits, every Iron entity is forged on the same foundation: unwavering will, deep faith, and selfless service.

We don't just build businesses. We build bridges.

In Memoriam

IronLimb Athletics

"He carried more than most will ever know — and he never put it down. This is his legacy."

— IronBridge Holdings

IronLimb Athletics is a non-profit athletic clothing brand created in honor of a dear friend — an amputee who lived with phantom limb syndrome. More than a brand, it is a mission: to fund research into phantom limb pain and to provide tangible support for amputees, especially our veterans.

Technology Venture

Forge App

Forge is an AI-powered fitness and wellness platform being developed under the IronBridge umbrella. More than a training app, Forge is designed to serve as the digital engine of the IronLimb community — connecting athletes, tracking progress, and translating community engagement into real-world impact.

Every Forge subscription drives a contribution toward phantom limb research. Every workout logged is a tribute. Technology with purpose.
