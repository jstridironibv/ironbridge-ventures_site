The Foundation

A Warrior's Legacy

IronLimb Foundation was founded by Jason Stridiron, US Navy Retired and Disabled Veteran, in memory of a dear friend — a civilian amputee who carried more than most will ever understand. He lived daily with phantom limb syndrome: the relentless, invisible pain of a limb that is no longer there. He faced it with iron will.

He was not a soldier. He was a brother. And like too many amputees, he turned to opioid pain medication because there was nothing better on offer. The medication became its own battle — one he ultimately did not win. His loss is not a footnote. It is the reason IronLimb exists.

Phantom limb syndrome is real, debilitating, and drastically underfunded. The inadequacy of non-opioid pain treatment options is not an inconvenience — it is costing lives. Every dollar raised, every partnership forged, every program funded carries his name forward.

"He carried more than most will ever know — and he never put it down. This foundation is his."

— Jason Stridiron, Founder · IronLimb Foundation

The Problem We Fight

Phantom Limb Syndrome

Phantom limb syndrome affects the overwhelming majority of amputees — a condition where the nervous system continues to send pain signals from a limb that no longer exists. It is chronic, debilitating, and drastically underfunded.

Veterans account for a significant and growing portion of the amputee population. Many face this condition without adequate support, research-backed treatment options, or community. That changes now.

What We Do

Four Pillars of Impact

🔬

Research Funding

Donations and program revenues directly fund phantom limb syndrome research. We partner with institutions working on neuroscience, mirror therapy, VR-based treatments, and prosthetic nerve integration. The science must catch up. We help fund the gap.

🎖️

Veteran Support

Amputee veterans deserve more than a thank you. IronLimb funds adaptive athletics programs, prosthetic access initiatives, and peer support networks specifically for veterans navigating life after limb loss. Service doesn't end when the uniform comes off.

💊

Non-Opioid Pain Research

The absence of viable non-opioid pain management for phantom limb syndrome is not a gap — it is a crisis. IronLimb Foundation funds and advocates for research into alternatives: neurostimulation, mirror therapy, VR treatment, and nerve-targeted approaches that don't carry opioid risk.

🤝

Community & Awareness

Phantom limb syndrome lives in the dark. IronLimb pulls it into the light — building a community of athletes, advocates, veterans, and allies who share stories, raise awareness, and refuse to let this condition remain invisible. Powered by the Enferno platform.
